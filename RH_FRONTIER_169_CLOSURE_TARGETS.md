# Frontier 169 — Six simultaneous proof targets

1. **Stieltjes/Pick:** derive an RH-independent positive-resolvent representation for H'/H directly from the theta kernel, including convergence and growth.
2. **Tau-Hankel:** derive all-order PSD Hankel matrices from a theta-specific cumulant-to-Gram/SOS identity.
3. **Jensen/Hermite:** prove the explicit cubic and then arbitrary-degree Hermite principal-minor inequalities; seek a uniform multi-copy Vandermonde/Binet–Cauchy factorization.
4. **de Branges:** construct an exact theta-defined Hermite–Biehler/canonical-system object and prove the determinant correspondence and self-adjointness.
5. **Li/Weil:** factor the explicit-formula quadratic form into a manifestly nonnegative theta/arithmetic SOS for every admissible test function.
6. **Nyman–Beurling/Báez–Duarte:** prove the required infinite-dimensional closure/coercivity estimate independently of RH.

## Global rule

A successful finite computation is a diagnostic only. A route closes only after a universal theorem with all analytic hypotheses proved and no use of the desired zero locations.
