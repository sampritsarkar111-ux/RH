# RH Frontier 118 — Log-Derivative Real-Axis Test — 2026-09-17

## New identity
For an entire function represented by a canonical product over its zeros rho,
\[
\frac{\Xi'(t)}{\Xi(t)}
\]
encodes the zero set through the corresponding partial-fraction expansion after the convergence factors are fixed. The proposed route is to compare this directly with the theta-side transform
\[
\Xi'(t)=-\int_{\mathbb R}u\Phi(u)\sin(tu)\,du.
\]
For real t, this is an exact identity from differentiation under the integral once dominated convergence is proved.

## Target
Construct a theta-side Herglotz/Stieltjes representation for a transformed logarithmic derivative, with a positive spectral measure supported on the real axis. If such a representation is established for the exact completed function and its poles are exactly the nontrivial zero parameters, then all those parameters are real.

## Crucial warning
A Herglotz representation must be derived from theta/modular structure. Merely defining a measure from the zeros would assume the desired conclusion. Likewise, positivity only on the real axis is insufficient unless the analytic continuation places the function in the appropriate Nevanlinna class.

## Closure gates
1. Exact transform and differentiation justification.
2. Correct variable transform from Xi to a candidate Herglotz function.
3. Positivity of its imaginary part in the upper half-plane, proved from theta data.
4. Representation theorem with a positive measure.
5. Identification of all poles with nontrivial zeros.
6. Proof that the support is real and no off-axis poles remain.

## Status
This is a new proof target, not a completed theorem. It is intended as an alternative to the all-degree Jensen wall.
