Even a complete proof of all degree-4 Hermite conditions would not establish RH. The final theorem must control every Hermite condition of every Jensen polynomial J_{d,n}.

## Obligation D — preserve the universal quantifiers

All statements must hold for every integer n>=0 and d>=1. Asymptotic ranges such as sufficiently large n for fixed d are insufficient.

## Obligation E — independent verification

Every claimed identity should be checked symbolically and, where useful, numerically. Numerical checks are diagnostics only and cannot serve as the universal proof.
