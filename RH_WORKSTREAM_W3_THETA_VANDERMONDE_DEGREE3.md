# W3 — Degree-3 Theta/Vandermonde Mechanism

## Objective

Take the monic cubic Jensen polynomial
\[
\widehat J(X)=X^3+aX^2+bX+c
\]
and its Hermite/Newton matrix. Substitute the exact theta-moment formulas for a,b,c and expand the quadratic form as multi-copy integrals.

## Exact gate

Determine whether the resulting integrand admits an identity of the form
\[
\sum_\alpha W_\alpha(u_1,\ldots,u_q)
\prod_{i<j}(u_i-u_j)^2
\]
with all residual weights nonnegative after the full theta/Poisson transformation.

## Rules

No pointwise claim based only on Phi(u)>0. No assumption that Jensen roots are real. No conjugation shortcut. Keep the unsummed theta lattice until the sign mechanism is explicit.

## Outcomes

- Positive exact factorisation: promote to a degree-uniform theorem.
- Signed residual: isolate it exactly and determine whether modular involution cancels it.
- Contradiction at finite degree: close the proposed Gram ansatz and pivot to W2/W4/W6.
