# Frontier 54 — Hard-core target after Frontiers 45–53

Date: 2026-09-16

The finite-degree algebra is now sufficiently reduced that further symbolic expansion alone is unlikely to close RH. The next decisive target is a genuinely Riemann-specific structural theorem.

## Target theorem

Prove one of the following equivalent-strength mechanisms:

1. The Jensen sequence generated by xi belongs to a class whose defining theorem implies hyperbolicity of every J_{d,n}.
2. The explicit theta kernel yields a total-positivity/PF-infinity statement strong enough to imply all Jensen hyperbolicity conditions.
3. A heat-flow invariant proves preservation of the complete Hermite positivity criterion at the physical xi parameter.
4. A degree-uniform sum-of-squares/determinantal identity proves every Hermite condition directly.

## Why this is the bottleneck

Frontier 40 rules out closure from generic positive Stieltjes moments. Frontiers 43, 46, 49, and 53 reduce the quartic layer to explicit finite algebra, but the remaining admissible-region constraint is not automatic. Frontier 44 and 45 show that even complete finite-degree closure would not satisfy the universal d,n quantifier.

## Required proof standard

Every new structural lemma must be stated with hypotheses, proved from the explicit Riemann object, and then propagated to all d,n. No numerical or asymptotic evidence may be promoted to a universal theorem.

## Status

This is the current hard-core mathematical target. RH remains unproved.
