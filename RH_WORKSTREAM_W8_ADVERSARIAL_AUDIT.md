# W8 — Adversarial Proof Audit

Every candidate lemma must be checked for:

1. hidden RH or real-zero assumptions;
2. invalid sum/integral/limit interchanges;
3. unjustified analytic continuation;
4. finite-degree-to-all-degree extrapolation;
5. sign errors in residues, determinants, factorial ratios, or Jacobians;
6. false pointwise positivity inferred from an integrated quantity;
7. accidental use of conjugation or modulus squares that presupposes zero reality;
8. accumulation at the transformed node 0;
9. loss of multiplicities;
10. normalization mismatch between Xi, theta, and Jensen conventions.

A proof claim passes only when each dependency is explicitly discharged.
