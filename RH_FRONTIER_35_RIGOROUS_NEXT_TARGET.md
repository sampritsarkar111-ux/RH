# Frontier 35 — next genuine closure target

Frontier 33 eliminated generic Stieltjes-moment positivity as a sufficient mechanism. Frontier 34 normalized the exact third Hermite obstruction to
\[
\Delta_3=\frac{d^3(d-1)^2(d-2)}{12}(dP_0-6H_0).
\]

The next target is now a concrete Riemann-theta calculation:

\[
\boxed{\text{Insert the exact theta-moment representation of }\gamma_m\text{ into }P_0,H_0\text{ and seek a positive-kernel identity.}}
\]

The desired endpoint is an identity of the form
\[
dP_0-6H_0=\int\!\cdots\int K_{d,n}(u_1,\ldots,u_k)\,d\mu(u_1)\cdots d\mu(u_k),
\]
with an explicitly nonnegative kernel \(K_{d,n}\), or an equivalent finite sum of squares. Merely proving positivity of the individual moments or their ordinary Hankel determinants is insufficient, by Frontier 33.

If no nonnegative kernel identity can be derived, the exact obstruction itself is retained as the current bottleneck; no RH conclusion is asserted.

**Status:** research target; not a proof.
