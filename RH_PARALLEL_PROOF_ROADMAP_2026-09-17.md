# Complete Parallel RH Proof Roadmap — 2026-09-17

## Status

The current project reduction is:
\[
\boxed{\mathrm{RH}\iff H_N=(\tau_{i+j})_{0\le i,j\le N}\succeq0\ \forall N.}
\]
The forward rigidity implication has been established conditionally on all-order PSD; the converse follows when RH holds because the transformed nodes are positive. The unresolved proof-bearing task is therefore an unconditional proof of all-order PSD, or an independent route to zero reality.

## Parallel tracks

### A. Exact analytic foundation
- Complete the zero-location-free Hadamard/Taylor derivation of \(\tau_n\).
- Prove all convergence, multiplicity, continuation, and limiting statements.
- Produce a canonical formula from completed Xi/theta data usable by every other track.

### B. Central Hankel positivity
- Prove \(Q_N(c)=\sum c_ic_j\tau_{i+j}\ge0\) for arbitrary \(N,c\).
- Seek an exact theta-derived Gram/SOS representation.
- Keep the theta lattice unsummed until positivity is exposed.

### C. Degree-3 breakthrough
- Explicitly compute the cubic Jensen/Hermite form.
- Expand into multi-copy theta integrals.
- Test exact Binet–Cauchy/Vandermonde-square pairings.
- Either obtain a positive certificate or record the exact residual obstruction.

### D. All-degree lift
- Generalize any successful degree-3 identity to arbitrary degree.
- Prove degree-uniform convergence and positivity.
- Avoid extrapolation from finitely many degrees.

### E. Stieltjes/J-fraction
- Derive Hankel determinants and Jacobi parameters from theta data.
- Test whether they have unconditional nonnegative signs.
- A complete sign theorem implies Hankel PSD.

### F. Jensen/Laguerre–Pólya
- Attack all-degree Jensen hyperbolicity directly.
- Establish the Pólya–Jensen passage with all hypotheses.
- Do not confuse positive coefficient moments with real-rootedness.

### G. de Branges/spectral route
- Construct a canonical system or de Branges function from theta.
- Prove kernel positivity/self-adjointness independently of RH.
- Prove exact Xi-zero/spectrum correspondence.

### H. de Bruijn–Newman route
- Derive exact heat-flow normalization.
- Seek a rigorous real-rootedness/parameter inequality at the required point.
- Do not replace proof by numerical estimates of the Newman constant.

### I. Equivalence/dependency audit
- Map all implications among Hankel PSD, Jensen hyperbolicity, Laguerre–Pólya, PF∞, Li-type criteria, spectral reality, and heat-flow criteria.
- Mark every arrow unconditional/conditional and prevent circular chains.

### J. Computational discovery
- Compute exact/high-precision Hankel minors, Jensen discriminants, theta integrals, residuals, and J-fraction coefficients.
- Use computation only to discover/falsify identities; convert every candidate to exact proof.

### K. Adversarial verification
- Audit every step for hidden RH, invalid interchange, wrong residue sign, normalization errors, accumulation at zero, finite-to-infinite extrapolation, and unjustified modulus-square arguments.

## Critical order

1. A + B + C in parallel.
2. E + F + G + H independently in parallel.
3. I continuously audits dependencies.
4. J supplies conjectures/falsifications only.
5. K audits every proposed lemma.
6. Assemble only after one unconditional proof-bearing chain survives K.

## Final completion criterion

Only the following establishes a complete proof:
\[
\text{theta/Xi identities}
\Rightarrow H_N\succeq0\ \forall N
\Rightarrow\text{Hamburger positivity}
\Rightarrow\text{Cauchy rigidity}
