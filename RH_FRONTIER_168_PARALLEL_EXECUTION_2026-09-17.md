## Parallel proof targets
1. **Pick/Stieltjes:** derive a theta-only global sign or positive-resolvent identity for the logarithmic derivative, retaining the full modular lattice until sign structure is explicit.
2. **tau-Hankel:** derive the exact cumulant transform from theta moments and prove PSD of every finite Hankel matrix by a theta-specific Gram/SOS identity.
3. **Jensen/Hermite:** prove hyperbolicity for every degree and shift through an all-degree theta-specific factorization; finite checks are diagnostic only.
4. **de Branges:** construct a theta-defined Hermite–Biehler/canonical system with self-adjointness and exact Xi determinant correspondence, without assuming real zeros.
5. **Li/Weil:** prove universal nonnegative quadratic forms directly from the theta/explicit formula for the full admissible test class.
6. **de Bruijn–Newman:** derive a genuine endpoint-rigidity theorem forcing the required endpoint, rather than restating RH as a condition on the constant.
7. **Laguerre–Pólya / total positivity:** seek an all-order structural theorem; explicitly reject generic PF∞ implications unsupported by the Xi/theta structure.
8. **Nyman–Beurling:** seek an unconditional arithmetic cyclicity/coercivity theorem, with the analytic equivalence audited independently.
9. **Adversarial audit:** for every candidate identity, test signs, low-order expansions, convergence, analytic continuation, and circular dependencies.

## Acceptance gate
Only a theorem that is unconditional and implies that every nontrivial Xi zero is real counts as RH closure. Otherwise record the exact obstruction and continue.

## Important research distinction
Numerical agreement, finite-order determinant positivity, or a paper claiming a proof is not itself a proof. External claims must be independently checked at the theorem and dependency level.
