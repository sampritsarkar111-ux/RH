# Frontier 175 — Literature Audit

SciSpace search returned several recent repository-indexed or posted works claiming proofs of RH. These claims are not accepted merely because their abstracts state that RH is proved.

## Audit rule

A claimed proof must expose a complete non-circular argument with every proof-bearing implication independently verifiable. In particular, symmetry of Xi, numerical zero checks, finite positivity, or a formal operator whose spectrum is asserted to match zeta zeros do not by themselves establish RH.

## Useful external directions

The search surfaced work involving spectral constructions, Beurling–Nyman criteria, Weil explicit formulas, Jensen/Hermite methods, and function-theoretic boundary-to-interior arguments. These are useful as candidate mechanisms, but each must be checked at its exact missing implication.

## Project decision

The current project remains explicitly **OPEN**. No literature claim is imported as a theorem without verification. The primary proof-bearing target remains an exact theta-specific positive-resolvent/Stieltjes/Pick certificate, with the other routes serving as independent reductions and falsification tests.
