# Frontier 176 — Parallel proof gates

The project now runs the following independent closure targets in parallel:

1. **Square-variable Stieltjes/Pick:** prove F(u)=-H'(sqrt(u))/(sqrt(u)H(sqrt(u))) is globally Stieltjes from theta data. This is a direct sufficient RH gate.
2. **Theta Hankel/SOS:** derive all-order positive Gram matrices directly from theta cumulants; finite PSD is not enough.
3. **Jensen/Hermite:** derive arbitrary-degree Jensen-polynomial hyperbolicity from the same positive object, not finite-degree numerics.
4. **de Branges:** construct an unconditional theta-defined canonical system whose characteristic determinant is Xi and prove self-adjointness.
5. **Li/Weil:** factor the full explicit-formula quadratic form into an unconditional positive square for every admissible test function.
6. **de Bruijn-Newman:** obtain endpoint rigidity that forces the required sign/value rather than assuming RH through Lambda.
7. **Nyman-Beurling/Báez-Duarte:** prove the required global density/coercivity estimate equivalent to RH.
8. **Cross-route bridge:** show whether a single positive spectral measure/operator simultaneously yields the Stieltjes, Hankel, Jensen and Weil positivity statements.

Acceptance criterion: only an unconditional theorem whose final logical step forces every nontrivial Xi zero to be purely imaginary (equivalently every zeta zero has real part 1/2) is a completed RH proof.
