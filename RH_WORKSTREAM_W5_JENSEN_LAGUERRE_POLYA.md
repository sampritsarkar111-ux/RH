# W5 — Jensen / Laguerre–Pólya Route

Starting from the exact theta coefficient representation, attack all-degree Jensen-polynomial hyperbolicity without assuming RH.

Targets:
1. derive the exact coefficient normalization;
2. establish finite-degree hyperbolicity by an independent positive kernel theorem, if possible;
3. prove degree-uniform control sufficient for the Pólya–Jensen limit;
4. connect the resulting Laguerre–Pólya property to the completed Xi function.

Positive theta moments alone are not enough: the missing ingredient is real-rootedness/hyperbolicity.
