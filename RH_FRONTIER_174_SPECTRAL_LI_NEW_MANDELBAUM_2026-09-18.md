# Frontier 174C — Spectral / Li-Weil / Newman / NB-BD Closure Ledger — 2026-09-18

## de Branges / Hilbert–Pólya
Required proof-bearing object: explicit theta-defined Hilbert space and self-adjoint operator or canonical system, with rigorous domain, spectrum, and determinant identity equal to Xi. A formally self-adjoint expression is insufficient. Weighted first-order ansatzes conjugate to free momentum and therefore do not supply the needed discrete spectrum.

## Li / Weil
Required: positivity of the full admissible Weil quadratic form, preferably via an explicit theta/arithmetic Gram/SOS factorization. Positivity of finitely many coefficients or a restricted test family is insufficient.

## de Bruijn–Newman
Required: an RH-independent proof of the endpoint inequality Lambda<=0. Known lower-bound information or finite deformation intervals does not close the endpoint.

## Nyman–Beurling / Baez–Duarte
Required: infinite-dimensional density/coercivity statement in the relevant Hilbert space. Finite-dimensional approximation or numerical decay does not imply exact closure.

## Status
All four remain OPEN. The objective is to turn each into a concrete theorem with an independently checkable load-bearing identity.
