# W7 — de Bruijn–Newman Route

Translate the completed theta kernel to the de Bruijn–Newman heat-flow family and identify an unconditional route to the required real-zero state at the Xi parameter.

Targets:
- derive the exact heat-flow normalization;
- state the parameter-to-zero theorem with all hypotheses;
- seek a rigorous finite-parameter real-rootedness theorem or an exact inequality implying the needed parameter bound;
- distinguish computational evidence from proof.

No numerical estimate of the Newman constant is to be promoted to a proof.
