# Frontier 60 audit note

The previous ledger entry described a zero-mode correction with a normalization-dependent coefficient. Directly from
\[
\vartheta_*(t)=\vartheta(t)-1,
\qquad
\vartheta(e^{-x})=e^{x/2}\vartheta(e^x),
\]
and
\[
\rho_+(x)=e^{x/4}\vartheta_*(e^x),
\]
one obtains
\[
\boxed{\rho_+(-x)-\rho_+(x)=2\sinh(x/4)}.
\]

Thus the canonical corrected even object for this normalization is
\[
\boxed{\rho_0(x)=\rho_+(x)-\sinh(x/4)}.
\]

This audit supersedes any earlier coefficient claim made with the same normalization. It does not establish positivity of \(\rho_0\), nor RH. The remaining proof obligation is a theta-specific sign-preserving certificate strong enough to imply all Jensen-polynomial Hermite inequalities.
