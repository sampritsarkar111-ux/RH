# Frontier 47 — rigorous progress assessment

## What is now proved inside the research program

1. The relevant Xi/Jensen coefficient route has been reduced to exact algebraic necessary conditions.
2. The first Turan obstruction has an exact theta-kernel representation.
3. The theta logarithmic moments admit an exact incomplete-Gamma parameter-derivative representation.
4. Inserting the theta series yields an exact double-sum formulation.
5. The raw Turan kernel is definitively not pointwise nonnegative; \(K_1(1,5)=-163612/5\) is an exact certificate.
6. The raw kernel cannot have a global \((x-y)^2\) factorization because its diagonal is not identically zero.
7. Low-order theta-weighted Turan values are numerically positive, but this remains non-proof evidence.
8. Higher Hermite analysis shows that generic positive Stieltjes moments do not supply the needed all-degree Jensen inequalities; the actual theta structure is indispensable.

## What is NOT proved

No theorem currently establishes the cubic cone \(4v^3+w^2\le0\) for every coefficient block. No theorem establishes all Jensen polynomials as hyperbolic. No theorem establishes all-order Toeplitz total positivity. Consequently there is no complete proof of RH in the repository at this stage.

## Quantitative interpretation

The phrase “more than 50% of the proof” is not mathematically well-defined: RH does not have a canonical percentage-completion metric. The defensible statement is that the project has closed several candidate mechanisms and converted the remaining work into explicit theorem gates, but the decisive global positivity/hyperbolicity gate remains open.

## Next highest-value target

The most valuable immediate target is a Riemann-specific proof of the degree-3 cubic cone from the theta representation, followed by a theorem that upgrades the same mechanism uniformly to every Jensen degree. Any proposed lemma must be tested against the exact kernel obstructions already recorded.
