# Frontier 150D–F — Independent Operator and Arithmetic Targets

## D — de Branges / canonical system
Construct a theta-defined canonical system or de Branges space whose characteristic determinant is exactly Xi. Prove self-adjointness, determinant convergence, and exact multiplicity-preserving zero correspondence. The construction must not encode real zeros by assumption.

## E — Li/Weil
Derive the explicit-formula quadratic form from theta/Mellin data and seek an RH-independent positive factorization valid for every admissible test function. Any use of an RH-equivalent positivity criterion without a new proof is circular.

## F — Nyman–Beurling
Prove the required density/cyclicity or coercivity statement directly from arithmetic/theta structure. Finite Gram positivity is not sufficient; the closure statement must be established.

## Shared acceptance gates
All three routes require exact analytic hypotheses, convergence/interchange proofs, and a final theorem forcing real Xi zeros. If a route only reproduces an RH equivalence, it is recorded as a reduction rather than a proof.
