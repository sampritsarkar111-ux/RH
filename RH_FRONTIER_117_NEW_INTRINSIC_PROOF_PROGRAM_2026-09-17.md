# RH Frontier 117 — Independent Intrinsic Proof Program — 2026-09-17

## Thesis
Do not attempt to force RH from generic positivity. Instead construct the zero geometry from an intrinsic self-adjoint spectral object generated by the completed theta kernel.

## Step 1 — Work with Xi directly
Use the exact even theta representation
\[
\Xi(t)=\int_{\mathbb R}\Phi(u)\cos(tu)\,du.
\]
The objective is to identify a canonical operator from Phi, not from observed zeros.

## Step 2 — Build a theta-derived quadratic form
For finite theta truncation Phi_N, define a symmetric bilinear form on polynomials or a weighted L2 space. Derive its matrix entries explicitly as theta moments. Determine whether modular inversion produces a hidden three-term relation after an appropriate orthogonalization.

## Step 3 — Seek an exact Jacobi operator
Gram-Schmidt the canonical monomial basis with respect to the theta-derived form. If the multiplication operator by the spectral variable is self-adjoint, its matrix in the orthogonal basis is Jacobi:
\[
J=\begin{pmatrix}
a_0&b_0&&\\b_0&a_1&b_1&\\&b_1&a_2&\ddots\\&&\ddots&\ddots
\end{pmatrix},\qquad b_k>0.
\]
The decisive task is to prove the Jacobi structure from theta/modular identities, not merely from an arbitrary positive measure.

## Step 4 — Identify Xi as a spectral determinant or characteristic limit
Prove an exact identity of the form
\[
\Xi(t)/\Xi(0)=\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac{\det(I-t^2J_N^{-1})}{1}
\]
or another rigorously normalized canonical determinant. Establish locally uniform convergence and equality with Xi.

## Step 5 — Zero geometry
Finite J_N has real spectrum. Prove that the limiting canonical product retains only real spectral parameters and that every nontrivial zero of zeta corresponds to one of them. This requires a zero-convergence theorem; it cannot be replaced by numerical agreement.

## Step 6 — Modular closure
The likely bottleneck is Step 3: theta positivity alone is too weak. The new target is an exact modular identity forcing the recurrence coefficients or a positivity-preserving evolution. Search for an involution under u -> -u combined with Poisson summation that produces a self-adjoint transfer operator.

## Step 7 — Adversarial tests
At every stage test against arbitrary positive measures and against truncated theta sums. Any lemma that survives only because Phi is positive but fails for the two-atom counterexample is not the missing Riemann-specific mechanism.

## Success criterion
A complete proof requires all operator-domain, determinant-convergence, modular-transform, and zero-identification steps. Until those are established, this document remains a research program rather than a proof.
