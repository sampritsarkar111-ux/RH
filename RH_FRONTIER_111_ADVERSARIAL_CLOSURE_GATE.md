# RH Frontier 111 — Adversarial Closure Gate

A purported unconditional RH proof must pass five gates:

1. **Theta/PF∞:** derive all-degree factorial/Jensen-normalized total positivity directly from the exact Riemann theta kernel. Raw Hankel positivity is insufficient.
2. **Nyman–Beurling:** prove arithmetic cyclicity/coercivity without assuming a zero-free region equivalent to RH. Finite Gram PSD and compact-support uniqueness are insufficient.
3. **Li/Weil:** produce a genuinely RH-independent positive factorization; rewriting Li/Weil positivity is not enough.
4. **Analytic legality:** justify every continuation, limit, sum/integral interchange, and determinant passage; no illegal use of divergent Dirichlet series.
5. **Infinite passage:** establish a uniform all-degree/order argument; finite computations cannot replace it.

## Current outcome

The project has substantial exact reductions, but no candidate currently passes all five gates. RH therefore remains open; the Clay Mathematics Institute currently lists it as an unsolved Millennium problem.
