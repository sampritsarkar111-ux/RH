# Reciprocal-coefficient log-concavity: generic route falsified

A tempting strategy is to try to prove `h_r^2 > h_{r-1} h_{r+1}` from positivity of the coefficients of `E(z)` alone, where

`1/E(-z)=sum h_n z^n`.

That implication is false in general.

One explicit positive coefficient sequence is

`e = (1, 19.85112559322549, 0.6820880410559178, 0.05583718932650969, 0.1523763211513484, 59.20577179696092, 0.01908710353691199, 49.52644287409105, 95.18661277619182, ...)`.

The reciprocal coefficients begin

`h_0=1`,
`h_1=19.85112559322549`,
`h_2=393.3850992769561`,
`h_3=7795.6526340710225`, ...

and at `r=2`,

`h_2^2-h_1 h_3 ≈ -0.6431869623775128 < 0`.

Therefore any proof of the required reciprocal log-concavity must exploit special analytic structure of the Riemann xi coefficients/kernel. Positivity of the coefficient sequence, and generic formal reciprocal identities, are insufficient.

This is a falsification of a proof route, not evidence against RH.
