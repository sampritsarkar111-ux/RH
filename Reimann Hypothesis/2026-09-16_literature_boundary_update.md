# 2026-09-16 — Literature boundary update

Current literature checked today confirms the central boundary rather than a closure.

1. Wojciech Michalowski, arXiv:2607.16795 (July 2026): strict positivity `D_{r,k}>0` for all `r>=2` and `k>=10^18 r^3`; the paper explicitly says the complementary region remains and does not prove RH.
2. Felix Cristiano Paim Kessler, Aug. 18 2026: strict positivity of the complete `k=1` family `D_{r,1}>0`; the construction uses verified critical-line zeros up to a finite height plus a controlled high-zero tail and explicitly does not prove RH.
3. Dimitrov–Lucas (2011): higher-order Turán inequalities establish hyperbolicity of degree-3 generalized Jensen polynomials, but this is finite-degree information rather than PF-infinity.
4. Wang–Yang (2024): Laguerre inequalities of arbitrary fixed order hold asymptotically for the Riemann Xi coefficients when `n > c r^3`; again this is fixed-order/asymptotic information.

These results reinforce the same exact closure problem: an all-orders, all-shifts positivity mechanism in the complementary region is still missing.

## Research consequence

The next attack should combine the two successful structural directions rather than repeat either one:

- Kessler's reciprocal-generating-function viewpoint for fixed shifts;
- Michalowski/Wang–Yang's `r^3` scale for large-order asymptotics;
- a new bridge that propagates positivity from the large-shift tail into the small-shift complementary wedge without assuming zero locations.

No RH proof is claimed.
