# Frontier 116 — de Branges / Li–Weil cross-route closure targets

Date: 2026-09-17

## de Branges

Construct a canonical system from theta data without using the unknown zero set. The proof-bearing identity must identify the spectral determinant exactly with \(\Xi\) (up to a nonvanishing explicit factor). Self-adjointness alone is insufficient unless this spectral identification is proved.

Target:
\[
\operatorname{Spec}(H_\theta)=\{t:\Xi(t)=0\}
\]
with \(H_\theta\) defined independently of those zeros.

## Li–Weil

Construct the exact Weil quadratic form from theta data and prove universal positivity on its complete admissible test class, or derive all Li coefficients as a positive norm plus explicitly nonnegative remainder. The bridge to RH must be the exact Li/Weil equivalence, not finite computation.

## Cross-route invariant

A common positive form is only a target until its Hilbert/test space, domain, maps, and equality of representations are proved. Record each proposed bridge as OPEN until those data are explicit.

## Completion criterion

Either route closes only when it yields a theorem that forces all zeros of \(\Xi\) to be real. Then the functional-equation correspondence gives the critical-line statement for nontrivial zeros of \(\zeta\).
