# RH Frontier 165 — Literature Audit — 2026-09-17

SciSpace search was run in parallel against peer-reviewed/research-indexed literature for theta, Pick/Herglotz/Stieltjes, Hankel positivity, de Branges/canonical systems, Li/Weil criteria, de Bruijn-Newman, and total positivity.

Key usable result: Li coefficients are a necessary and sufficient criterion for RH; positivity of all Li coefficients is equivalent to RH. A 2012 canonical-system paper constructs a system associated with the xi-function unconditionally for a restricted parameter range and identifies extension to all positive parameters as a criterion related to RH.

Important audit rule: repository-indexed papers claiming a complete RH proof are not accepted merely because their abstracts claim resolution. They require independent verification of every central theorem, especially positivity and spectral/determinant identities.

This audit reinforces the project's proof standard: the missing step is an unconditional, independently checkable positivity/spectral identity, not another reformulation of RH.
