# Frontier 174B — All-Order Jensen/Hermite and Hankel Gate — 2026-09-18

## Target
Prove a structural all-degree/all-shift hyperbolicity theorem for Jensen polynomials of Xi, or an equivalent total-positivity/variation-diminishing theorem, strong enough to force Xi into the Laguerre–Pólya class.

## Exact bridge
Finite factorial-Hankel PSD is implied by a Stieltjes logarithmic derivative, but the converse requires moment-support and global analytic conditions. Thus finite determinants cannot be promoted to RH.

## Strongest structural candidate
Seek a theta-kernel representation whose coefficient arrays are totally positive, then use a Binet–Cauchy factorization to prove every Jensen/Hermite principal minor is nonnegative. The factorization must be uniform in degree and shift.

## Degree-4 checkpoint
Any degree-4 certificate is only a local algebraic test. The terminal theorem must cover all degrees, not just a finite list.
