# RH Frontier 137 — Unsummed Theta Kernel Attack on the Pick Inequality

Date: 2026-09-17

## 1. Correct normalization

Use the standard completed Riemann Xi-function in the Fourier variable `t`, with

`Xi(t) = ∫_0^∞ Φ(u) cos(tu) du`,

where

`Φ(u) = Σ_{n≥1} (4π² n⁴ e^{9u/2} - 6π n² e^{5u/2}) e^{-π n² e^{2u}}`.

For `u≥0`, each summand is positive because

`4π²n⁴e^{9u/2}-6πn²e^{5u/2} = 2πn²e^{5u/2}(2πn²e^{2u}-3) > 0`.

Define

`H(z) = Xi(i√z) = ∫_0^∞ Φ(u) cosh(u√z) du`.

Because Xi is even, H is entire in z. Under RH, a zero `ρ=1/2+iγ` maps to `z=-γ²`, so the desired zero geometry is the negative real axis.

The natural logarithmic derivative is therefore

`F(z) = H'(z)/H(z)`,

not `-H'/H`: on positive real z, H is increasing and F(z)>0. The Stieltjes target is

`F(z) = a + ∫_0^∞ dν(t)/(z+t)`.

## 2. Exact unsummed derivative

Let `q=√z`, with the analytic branch on the slit plane. Then

`H(z) = ∫ Φ(u) cosh(qu) du`,

and

`H'(z) = ∫ Φ(u) [u sinh(qu)/(2q)] du`.

Hence

`F(z) = [∫ Φ(u) u sinh(qu)/(2q) du] / [∫ Φ(u) cosh(qu) du]`.

No zeros have been introduced in this formula.

## 3. Exact double-integral formula for the Pick sign

For `Im z>0`, write

`D(z)=H'(z) \overline{H(z)}`.

Then

`Im F(z) = Im D(z)/|H(z)|²`.

Substitution of the unsummed theta integral gives the exact identity

`Im F(z) = (1/(2i |H(z)|²)) ∫_0^∞∫_0^∞ Φ(u)Φ(v) K_q(u,v) du dv`,

where

`K_q(u,v) = [u/(2q)] sinh(qu) cosh(conj(q)v) - [v/(2conj(q))] cosh(qu) sinh(conj(q)v)`.

The double-integral is purely imaginary after integration because exchanging `u,v` gives the conjugate-negative contribution. Thus the desired Pick inequality is exactly the global sign problem

`Im z>0  ==>  (1/(2i))∫∫ Φ(u)Φ(v)K_q(u,v)du dv ≤ 0`.

This is the first direct theta-kernel form of the missing inequality.

## 4. Immediate obstruction: pointwise positivity fails

The kernel `K_q(u,v)` is not pointwise sign-definite. For example, at `q=1+0.5i`, its imaginary part is positive at `(u,v)=(0.2,1)` but negative at `(1,1)`. Therefore the proof cannot be obtained by simply proving `Im K_q(u,v)≤0` term-by-term.

This is important: the required sign, if true, must come from a nonlocal property of the full theta weight `Φ`, including its modular structure and the coupling of all `(u,v)`.

## 5. What theta positivity does prove immediately

Since `Φ(u)>0`, for real `x>0`,

`H(x)>0`, `H'(x)>0`,

so

`F(x)>0`.

Moreover the Taylor expansion at zero is

`H(z)=Σ_{n≥0} a_n z^n`,

with

`a_n = [1/(2n)!]∫_0^∞ Φ(u)u^{2n}du >0`.

But positive coefficients of H do not by themselves imply the Pick property of `H'/H`.

## 6. The stronger exact target

A successful proof must derive one of the following directly from the unsummed theta expression:

### Target A — Pick kernel identity

Find a decomposition of the integrated antisymmetric kernel into

`(1/(2i))∫∫ Φ(u)Φ(v)K_q(u,v)du dv = -P(z)`,

where `P(z)≥0` for `Im z>0`.

### Target B — positive spectral factorization

Construct an explicit theta-derived Hilbert-space map `V_z` and positive operator `A≥0` such that

`Im F(z) = - Im(z) ⟨V_z,(A-z)^{-1}V_z⟩ / normalization`.

Then the Pick sign follows from the resolvent identity without any zero-location input.

### Target C — total-positivity mechanism

Use the exact modular identity of Φ to prove that the bilinear form generated by `K_q` is negative semidefinite, even though the pointwise kernel is indefinite.

## 7. New useful reduction

The problem has therefore been reduced from an abstract RH criterion to a concrete theta-only statement:

`Θ modular identity + Φ(u)>0`

must imply the global negativity of the explicitly displayed quadratic/double integral above.

A proof of this global sign would establish the Pick property of `F`; the Stieltjes representation would then force the zeros of H onto `(-∞,0]`, which is equivalent to the critical-line condition for the nontrivial zeta zeros.
