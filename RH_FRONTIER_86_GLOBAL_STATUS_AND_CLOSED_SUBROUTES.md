Date: 2026-09-16

## Required standard for closure

No route is accepted unless it supplies an unconditional theorem for every finite degree/shift, proves all limiting interchanges and convergence claims, handles equality/degenerate cases, and ends with an explicit non-circular implication to RH.

The repository contains substantial exact reductions and adversarial eliminations, but no complete RH proof. Frontier 85 identifies the narrowest remaining FEATAM-derived target as a theta-specific stability-preserving operator theorem.
