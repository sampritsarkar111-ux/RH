# Frontier 171 — Master Identity Attacks — 2026-09-17

## Objective
Attack the remaining proof-bearing identities directly rather than merely restating equivalent criteria.

## A. Stieltjes/Pick master identity
Let Xi(w)=xi(1/2+w), H(z)=Xi(sqrt(z)). Seek

H'(z)/H(z)=a+∫_[0,∞) dμ(t)/(z+t),  μ≥0.

Required sublemmas:
1. H'/H is single-valued and holomorphic on C\(-∞,0].
2. Boundary imaginary part has the Stieltjes sign.
3. H'/H-a vanishes appropriately at infinity.
4. Herglotz/Stieltjes representation theorem applies with a positive measure.
5. The construction comes directly from theta/arithmetic data and does not use zero locations.

Circularity test: defining μ from the unknown zeros is forbidden.

## B. Tau-Hankel identity
For m(x)=-H'(x)/H(x), test whether

(-1)^n m^(n)(x) ≥ 0

can be upgraded from finite numerical evidence to complete monotonicity through a theta-specific SOS/Gram identity. Generic moment positivity is insufficient because logarithmic cumulants are nonlinear in the moments.

## C. Jensen/Hermite
RH requires every Jensen polynomial J^(d,n) to be hyperbolic. Eventual hyperbolicity is insufficient. The actual closure target is a uniform all-d,n theorem, preferably by an explicit total-positivity or Binet–Cauchy factorization.

## D. de Branges
Construct a theta-defined Hermite–Biehler/canonical-system object for all required parameter range. Prove:

exact characteristic determinant = Xi,
self-adjointness,
correct spectrum,
and no RH-dependent definition.

## E. Li/Weil
The target is positivity of the full Weil quadratic form for the complete admissible test class, not finite truncations. Search for an exact theta/arithmetic SOS factorization with every boundary term accounted for.

## F. de Bruijn–Newman
RH is equivalent to Λ≤0 while known results prove Λ≥0. Therefore the missing statement is exact endpoint control. A successful route must prove Λ≤0 directly from theta estimates without smuggling RH into the endpoint hypothesis.

## G. Nyman–Beurling/Baez–Duarte
The target is infinite-dimensional density/coercivity, not finite approximation error. Any proof must identify the precise norm and closure mechanism and verify it independently of RH.

## H. Symmetry obstruction retained
Functional equation + Schwarz symmetry + reality on the critical line do not force zero location; the polynomial counterexample from Frontier 170 is a permanent anti-circularity test.

## Acceptance gate
Only an unconditional universal theorem implying every nontrivial zero rho satisfies Re(rho)=1/2 counts as a completion.
