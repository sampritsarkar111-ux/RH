# W29 — All-Order Determinant Test

Derive a symbolic recurrence or closed form for the principal Hankel determinants
\[
\Delta_N=\det(\tau_{i+j})_{0\le i,j\le N}.
\]

Seek a theta-lattice formula with manifestly nonnegative terms, a determinant recurrence with positive coefficients, or an exact obstruction. This is a direct test of the central RH-equivalent gate and may expose the missing structural invariant.
