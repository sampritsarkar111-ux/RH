# Frontier 150B — All-Order tau-Hankel Target

Define the tau sequence from the logarithmic derivative of the square-root Xi normalization. The project has identified that theta Taylor moments and tau coefficients are related by a nonlinear cumulant transform.

## Exact target
For every N>=0 and every real vector c=(c_0,...,c_N), prove

Q_N(c)=sum_{i,j=0}^N c_i c_j tau_{i+j} >= 0

from the completed Riemann theta structure alone.

## Required mechanism
Derive the cumulant transform exactly, then seek a Riemann-specific representation of Q_N as a finite/infinite sum of manifestly nonnegative terms. Generic moment positivity is insufficient because nonlinear cumulants can destroy Hankel positivity.

## Audit gates
- No RH-dependent zero measure.
- No assumed real zeros.
- Every finite-N identity exact.
- Infinite lattice limits locally/uniformly controlled.
- If positivity fails at any finite N, record the exact obstruction.

## Consequence
An unconditional all-order tau-Hankel theorem can feed the existing Cauchy/Hamburger rigidity route; the final implication to real Xi zeros must still be checked separately.
