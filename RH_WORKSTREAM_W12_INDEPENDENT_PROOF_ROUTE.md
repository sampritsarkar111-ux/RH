# W12 — Independent Route / Failure Protocol

If the central Hankel route stalls, pursue an independent theorem rather than weakening the gate.

Candidate structures: de Branges canonical systems, self-adjoint spectral realizations, de Bruijn–Newman heat flow, Pólya–Jensen hyperbolicity, or an exact theta-derived total-positivity theorem.

For each route record:
1. exact input theorem;
2. new lemma required;
3. whether the lemma is genuinely stronger than an RH equivalent;
4. whether the proof is zero-location-free;
5. exact point where the route succeeds or fails.

A route that merely restates RH in another language is not a proof.
