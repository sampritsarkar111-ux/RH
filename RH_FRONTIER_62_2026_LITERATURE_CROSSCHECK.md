# Frontier 62 — 2026 literature cross-check and route selection

Date: 2026-09-16

## Findings incorporated into the research program

A September 2026 search located several relevant 2026 preprints/results.

1. Jonathan Holland, arXiv:2608.08682, proves a simultaneous hyperbolicity wedge of the form n^3 log^2(n+2) >= K d^5. This is a substantial uniform result but leaves an infinite finite-strip regime, so it is not RH.

2. A 2026 preprint by Planat studies the finite-strip obstruction and explicitly states that its work does not prove RH; it reports asymptotic hyperbolicity and certified finite-degree/finite-shift results while identifying a remaining non-perturbative obstruction.

3. A 2026 preprint by Gershon claims Lambda=0 using strict log-concavity, finite Jensen-polynomial certification, and a global dissipation argument. Because the work is a preprint and its crucial global passage must be independently checked, it is treated here as an external claim to audit, not as an established proof.

4. Another 2026 preprint explicitly emphasizes the TP2 versus TP-infinity gap: log-concavity of the Riemann kernel is weaker than the infinite-order total-positivity property required for a direct Laguerre-Pólya conclusion.

5. A separate 2026 GitHub research landscape reports a claimed failure of PF5 for the de Bruijn-Newman kernel. This is relevant as a warning against assuming ordinary kernel PF-infinity from low-order positivity; the exact source and conventions must be independently audited before using the claim.

## Strategic consequence

The most promising next target is no longer simply another low-degree Jensen discriminant. The research should run two parallel routes:

A. **Global kernel route:** determine whether a valid Riemann-specific spectral/heat-flow/dissipation inequality can bridge finite-order positivity to Lambda=0 without assuming PF-infinity.

B. **Jensen/Hermite route:** use degrees 5+ as falsification tests for any proposed universal centered-invariant recurrence, while seeking a structural theorem that controls all degrees simultaneously.

## Proof-status rule

No external preprint claim is imported as a proof. Every global inequality must be independently derived or independently verified with all quantifiers and analytic hypotheses exposed.
