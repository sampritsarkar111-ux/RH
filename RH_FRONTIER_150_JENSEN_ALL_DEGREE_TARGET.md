# Frontier 150C — All-Degree Jensen/Hermite Target

For factorial-normalized Xi coefficients gamma_n, the Jensen family is

J_{d,n}(X)=sum_{j=0}^d binom(d,j) gamma_{n+j} X^j.

## Exact target
Prove hyperbolicity of J_{d,n} for every d,n>=0 directly from the completed Riemann theta lattice, without assuming RH.

## Proposed mechanism
Expand the discriminant/Hermite forms through d-copy or higher-copy theta integrals. Seek an exact Binet–Cauchy/Vandermonde-square decomposition whose sign is forced by Riemann-specific modular/arithmetic pairing rather than generic positive-measure arguments.

## Required checks
1. Re-derive degree 2 with the corrected factorial ratio.
2. Re-derive degree 3 and retain the exact four-copy residual.
3. Identify the first genuinely theta-specific sign constraint not implied by ordinary moment positivity.
4. Generalize the structure to arbitrary d.
5. Prove convergence and the d,n-uniform limiting passage.
6. Connect all-degree hyperbolicity to the real-zero class only after its hypotheses are independently verified.

## Anti-circularity
Never write zeros as 1/2+it before the all-degree theorem is established. Numerical hyperbolicity is not a proof.
