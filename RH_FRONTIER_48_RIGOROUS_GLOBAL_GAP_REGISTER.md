# Frontier 48 — Rigorous global gap register

Date: 2026-09-16

## What is now established

1. RH can be attacked through universal hyperbolicity of the Jensen polynomials J_{d,n}.
2. The first Hermite layer gives adjacent Turan inequalities.
3. The degree-3 layer reduces exactly to the centered cubic discriminant cone.
4. The degree-4 layer reduces exactly to a centered quartic discriminant polynomial F(y,t).
5. Ratio/finite-difference coordinates remove normalization ambiguity.
6. Generic positive Stieltjes moment positivity is insufficient; explicit finite atomic counterexamples exist.

## What is NOT established

A proof that the Riemann theta kernel forces the cubic cone for every n.

A proof that the same kernel forces the quartic admissible-region condition for every n.

A degree-uniform theorem controlling every higher Hermite minor.

An independent theorem converting any one of the above finite-degree properties into RH without the universal Jensen-hyperbolicity requirement.

## Non-negotiable closure condition

The final proof must establish

J_{d,n} is hyperbolic for every integer d,n >= 0.

Any argument stopping at finite d, asymptotic n, numerical zeros, or a conditional implication remains incomplete.

## Research protocol

Every new candidate structural inequality is to be checked in this order:

(1) exact symbolic derivation;
(2) counterexample search over generic positive measures;
(3) specialization to the explicit Riemann theta kernel;
(4) proof of the Riemann-specific hypothesis;
(5) universal quantifier over d,n;
(6) independent verification.

## Status

The project has advanced through exact algebraic reductions, but the central universal Riemann-specific closure theorem remains open. No RH proof is claimed.
