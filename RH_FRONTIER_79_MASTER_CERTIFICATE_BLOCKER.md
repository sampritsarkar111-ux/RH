# Frontier 79 — master certificate blocker

Date: 2026-09-16

## Result of the attempted unification
The proposed master Hermite quadratic-form certificate remains a target rather than an identity. The exact theta/incomplete-Gamma representation supplies the coefficient data, but no derivation has been found that makes every finite Hermite form a sum of positive squares uniformly in degree and shift.

## Why this matters
A schematic SOS expression is not evidence of RH unless every kernel, measure, coefficient, convergence statement, and equality case is explicitly derived. In particular, the sign-indefinite low-degree theta kernels found earlier prevent replacing the missing derivation with a generic moment-positivity assertion.

## Surviving exact target
Find explicit theta-derived objects \(P_{\alpha,j}\), positive measures \(\mu_\alpha\), and a proof that
\[
c^T\mathsf H_n^{(d)}c
=\sum_\alpha\int\left|\sum_jc_jP_{\alpha,j}\right|^2d\mu_\alpha
\]
for every finite \(d,n\). If obtained, this would imply all Jensen polynomials are hyperbolic and would close the RH route.

## Status
No such global identity has been proved in this pass. RH remains unproved.
