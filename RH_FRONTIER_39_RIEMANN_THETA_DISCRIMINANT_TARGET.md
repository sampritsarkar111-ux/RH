# Frontier 39 — Riemann-theta discriminant closure attempt

## Objective

The Jensen/Hermite route reduces RH to hyperbolicity of every generalized Jensen polynomial. For the leading 3x3 Hermite minor, the normalized obstruction can be written in centered-moment coordinates. The next attempt is to connect that exact condition directly to the Riemann theta kernel rather than to generic moment positivity.

## 1. Exact cubic criterion

For a monic cubic
\[
p(x)=x^3+b_1x^2+b_2x+b_3,
\]
the determinant of its 3x3 Hermite matrix equals the discriminant
\[
\operatorname{Disc}(p)=b_1^2b_2^2-4b_2^3-4b_1^3b_3-27b_3^2+18b_1b_2b_3.
\]
Thus \(\Delta_3\ge0\) is necessary for three real roots and, together with the lower Hermite conditions, is part of an exact hyperbolicity certificate.

For the degree-3 Jensen polynomial, the normalized condition derived previously is
\[
\boxed{w^2+4v^3\le0},
\]
where \(v\) is the normalized second centered moment and \(w\) the normalized third centered moment of the five consecutive coefficient data after the appropriate normalization.

## 2. Theta-kernel representation

The Riemann-xi Taylor coefficients admit a theta-kernel moment representation of the form
\[
\gamma_m=C_0\frac{m!}{(2m)!}M_m,
\qquad
M_m=\int_0^\infty u^{2m}\Phi(u)\,du,
\]
with \(C_0>0\) and the standard Riemann theta kernel \(\Phi\). Therefore every finite block \((M_n,\ldots,M_{n+4})\) is generated by one fixed positive kernel.

The important point is that the required Jensen inequality is not a consequence of positivity of an arbitrary Stieltjes moment sequence: Frontier 33 supplied an exact positive two-atom counterexample after applying the same factorial multiplier. Hence the proof must exploit the special analytic form of \(\Phi\).

## 3. Exact closure target

Define
\[
D_n^{(2)}=M_nM_{n+2}-M_{n+1}^2,
\]
and higher Hankel minors analogously. The immediate target is an identity or inequality of the form
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal Q_d(\gamma_{n+d},\ldots,\gamma_{n+d-4})
=\mathcal S_{d,n}[\Phi]+\mathcal R_{d,n}[\Phi],
}
\]
where \(\mathcal S_{d,n}[\Phi]\) is manifestly nonnegative and the remainder can be bounded using an explicit theta-kernel identity.

A successful identity must be exact, finite-degree independent, and contain no assumption equivalent to RH.

## 4. Attempted route: generic moment positivity

This route fails. The determinant of a generic Hankel moment matrix is nonnegative for a positive measure, but the Jensen Hermite determinant is a different polynomial in consecutive moments because of the factorial coefficient transformation. Therefore total positivity of the raw moment matrix does not automatically imply positivity of the Jensen Hermite matrix.

This is a genuine obstruction, not merely a missing estimate.

## 5. Stronger candidate: kernel-level total positivity

A viable new mechanism would be to prove that the transformed coefficient kernel
\[
K_n(t)=\frac{n!}{(2n)!}t^n\Phi(\sqrt t)
\]
induces a strictly sign-regular family of the order required by all Jensen Hermite minors. Such a theorem would need to be established directly from the theta kernel, not assumed from general Stieltjes theory.

The decisive finite-dimensional test is the exact cubic inequality above. If it can be written as an integral of a pointwise nonnegative kernel expression, that would provide the first genuinely Riemann-specific positivity certificate.

## 6. Current result

No complete RH proof has been obtained in this frontier. What has been established is a sharpened proof target: generic moment positivity is insufficient, and the missing theorem must exploit special theta-kernel structure or another genuinely Riemann-specific mechanism.

**Status: partial — exact target isolated, no unsupported RH conclusion.**
