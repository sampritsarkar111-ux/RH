Date: 2026-09-16

This frontier consolidates the active proof attempt.

## Exact route under test

1. Establish the xi/Jensen-polynomial equivalence.
2. Derive a Riemann-specific structural theorem from the theta representation or an equivalent global analytic representation.
3. Use that theorem to control all centered Jensen invariants uniformly in n and d.
4. Prove all Hermite-matrix conditions (including degeneracies) for every J_{d,n}.
5. Conclude hyperbolicity for every d,n.
6. Invoke the exact Jensen-polynomial equivalence to conclude RH.

## Breakthrough criterion

A new lemma counts as a genuine breakthrough only if it supplies a previously missing implication that is exact, universally quantified, and independently checkable. Low-degree algebra, numerical agreement, asymptotic fixed-degree results, and reformulations do not count as closure.
