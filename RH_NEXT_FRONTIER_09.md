# Riemann Hypothesis — Frontier 09: Stress-Test of the Moment Inequality

Date: 2026-09-16

## Target

The corrected degree-2 Jensen condition is
\[
M_{n+1}^2\ge\frac{2n+1}{2n+3}M_nM_{n+2}.
\]

## Stress test

For an arbitrary positive measure, Cauchy–Schwarz gives
\[
M_{n+1}^2\le M_nM_{n+2}.
\]
Thus the desired lower bound lies strictly below the generic upper bound. Positivity of the measure is therefore not enough to decide the inequality.

More strongly, positive measures can make the ratio \(M_{n+1}^2/(M_nM_{n+2})\) arbitrarily small: a two-point measure with a very small mass at a very large point provides such examples. Therefore no proof can replace the special Riemann theta kernel by an arbitrary positive measure.

## Consequence

The required argument must exploit specific structure of the Riemann kernel — for example its exact theta-series form, complete monotonicity, total positivity, or another rigid variation-diminishing property. A generic moment theorem cannot finish this step.

## Status

This rules out a tempting but invalid shortcut. The degree-2 inequality remains open in this research branch, and the all-degree Jensen problem remains substantially stronger.
