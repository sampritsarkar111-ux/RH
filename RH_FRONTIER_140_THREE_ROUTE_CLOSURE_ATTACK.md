# RH Frontier 140 — Three-Route Closure Attack

Date: 2026-09-17

## Objective

Attack the three remaining walls independently rather than repeatedly testing the same four-index theta determinant:

1. theta -> tau Hankel positivity;
2. global Pick/Stieltjes sign;
3. equivalent Xi growth positivity.

No route may use the locations of the zeta zeros as an input.

## Route A — cumulant/Hankel transform

Let

`H(z)=sum_{k>=0} h_k z^k = Xi(sqrt(z))`,

with `Xi(w)=xi(1/2+w)`. The theta representation gives

`h_k = 2(-1)^k M_k/(2k)!`.

The zero moments are generated by

`tau_n = -(1/n!) [d^n/dz^n](H'/H)(0)`

in the convergent derivative range after canonical-product normalization.

Therefore the bridge is a nonlinear map from the theta Taylor sequence to logarithmic cumulants. The first coefficients are

`[z^0] H'/H = h_1/h_0`,

`[z^1] H'/H = (h_0 h_2-h_1^2)/h_0^2`,

`[z^2] H'/H = (h_0^2 h_3-3h_0h_1h_2+2h_1^3)/h_0^3`.

A key adversarial test follows: raw theta Hankel positivity does not algebraically imply positivity of these cumulant Hankel matrices. Thus a successful Route A needs a new closure theorem, such as a Stieltjes moment theorem for the logarithmic derivative, not a determinant identity based only on `M_k`.

## Route B — Pick/Stieltjes attack

Set `F=H'/H`. A sufficient RH theorem is

`Im z>0 => Im F(z)<=0`,

plus the standard Stieltjes growth/normalization conditions.

From

`H(z)=2 integral_0^infinity Phi(u) cos(u sqrt(z))du`,

`H'(z)=- integral_0^infinity Phi(u)u sin(u sqrt(z))/sqrt(z)du`,

we obtain an exact double-integral formula for the Pick numerator. The one-variable theta factor `Phi` is positive, but the antisymmetrized complex kernel changes sign pointwise. Therefore pointwise positivity is impossible as the closure mechanism.

The remaining proof-bearing alternatives are:

* modular/Poisson pairing of the full `(m,n)` lattice;
* a positive square-modulus factorization after four-copy symmetrization;
* a direct resolvent representation generated by the theta operator.

Any claimed pointwise kernel inequality must be rejected unless its sign is proved globally.

## Route C — growth criterion / paired-block attack

For the standard real-even Xi function `Xi(t)=xi(1/2+it)`, the logarithmic derivative has equivalent positivity criteria associated with growth of `|Xi(x+iy)|`. The theta representation yields a two-variable integral after differentiating the modulus square. Recent preprint literature reports partial paired-tail decompositions but explicitly leaves global pre-crest positivity unresolved; therefore this route is not closed by existing partial calculations.

The exact target is to derive an all-block identity

`G(x,y)=sum_m [Q_m(x,y)+R_m(x,y)]`,

where `Q_m>=0` and either `R_m>=0` or the full residual telescopes/cancels exactly. The key adversarial requirement is uniformity in all real x and y>0.

## Current hard boundary

The three routes meet at the same mathematical object: the zero geometry of H. The theta kernel supplies strong positivity at the raw-moment/TP2 level, but a proof of RH requires a nonlinear positivity principle strong enough to force the entire function into the Laguerre-Pólya class, or an equivalent Pick/Stieltjes/growth theorem.

A 2026 preprint reports strict log-concavity (TP2) of the Riemann Xi kernel, while explicitly stating that passage to TP-infinity/Laguerre-Pólya remains open. Thus TP2 cannot legitimately be promoted to RH.
