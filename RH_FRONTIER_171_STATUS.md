# Frontier 171 Status

Parallel proof-engine audit completed on 2026-09-17.

## Result
No complete RH proof was produced. The current frontier is now organized around explicit master identities and their necessary analytic sublemmas.

## Key mathematical blockers
- Stieltjes/Pick: positivity and representation of H'/H remain unproved from RH-independent theta data.
- Tau-Hankel: finite PSD tests do not establish all-order Gram/SOS positivity.
- Jensen/Hermite: finite or eventual hyperbolicity does not imply all-degree/all-shift hyperbolicity.
- de Branges: no verified RH-independent canonical system with exact Xi determinant has been constructed.
- Li/Weil: no universal nonnegative factorization for the complete admissible test class has been derived.
- de Bruijn-Newman: endpoint equality/inequality sufficient for RH remains unresolved.
- Nyman-Beurling/Baez-Duarte: required infinite-dimensional closure/coercivity remains unresolved.

## Computational note
Numerical positivity is retained as diagnostic evidence only; it is not a proof and is vulnerable to conditioning/truncation issues.

## Anti-circularity requirement
No definition of a positive measure, self-adjoint operator, or spectral object may use the unknown zero locations in its construction.

## Completion criterion
A route may be marked COMPLETE only after a rigorous unconditional theorem implies Re(rho)=1/2 for every nontrivial zero rho.
