# Frontier 69 — Heat-flow global closure target

Date: 2026-09-16
Status: hard target; no proof claimed

The alternative endgame is to prove the de Bruijn-Newman constant satisfies Λ≤0. Together with the established lower bound Λ≥0, this would force Λ=0 and hence RH.

The required construction is a global Lyapunov functional L(λ) for the deformed Riemann xi function H_λ such that:

1. L is finite and well-defined for every λ in the relevant half-line;
2. its derivative along heat flow has a rigorously fixed sign;
3. all zero-collision and multiple-zero cases are handled;
4. theta-series tails and differentiation/interchange limits are uniform;
5. the sign information actually propagates to λ=0 rather than only to large positive λ.

A candidate based only on pairwise zero repulsion is insufficient unless it controls the entire infinite zero configuration and the boundary terms.

Parallel target: identify whether a finite-dimensional Hermite/Jensen inequality can be interpreted as a heat-flow monotonicity statement. Such a bridge could convert the all-degree Jensen problem into a single global functional inequality.

No numerical estimate of Λ is accepted as a proof of Λ≤0.
