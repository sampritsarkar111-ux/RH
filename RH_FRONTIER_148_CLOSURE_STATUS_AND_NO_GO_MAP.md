## Unified missing theorem

A zero-location-free theta spectral transfer that simultaneously provides:

1. a positive Weyl/Herglotz boundary value;
2. a positive spectral measure with the correct positive-pole geometry;
3. a square/Vandermonde factorization of the Pick numerator;
4. total positivity of the induced cumulant coordinates.

If this theorem is proved, the project can reconnect the Pick and tau-Hankel routes to the standard Laguerre-Pólya/Jensen implication. Until then, RH remains unproved.

## Important methodological rule

Repeated finite-degree numerical positivity is not a substitute for an all-order identity. Every claimed closure must be an exact symbolic statement with convergence, positivity, and RH-independence proved.
