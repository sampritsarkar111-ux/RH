# W4 — Stieltjes/J-fraction Route

Study \(F(z)=\sum_{n\ge0}\tau_nz^n\) as a candidate Stieltjes moment transform. Derive Hankel determinants and Jacobi/J-fraction coefficients directly from theta/Xi data.

Target:
\[
F(z)=\cfrac{a_0}{1-b_0z-\cfrac{a_1z^2}{1-b_1z-\cdots}},\qquad a_k\ge0,
\]
with exact convergence/domain statements. Prove whether theta structure forces these signs unconditionally.

A successful all-order nonnegative coefficient theorem implies Hankel positivity. If signs fail, record the first exact obstruction.
