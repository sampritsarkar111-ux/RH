# Frontier 47 — Riemann-specific closure routes

Date: 2026-09-16

## Purpose

The generic moment route is blocked. The remaining research must exploit structure special to the Riemann xi function.

## Route A: theta-kernel total positivity

Start from Xi(t)=integral_0^infinity Phi(u) cos(tu) du and its explicit theta-derived kernel. Seek a proof that the associated transformed coefficient sequence belongs to a sufficiently strong Pólya-frequency / total-positivity class that implies every Jensen polynomial is hyperbolic.

The exact implication needed is stronger than ordinary Stieltjes moment positivity.

## Route B: heat flow

Use the de Bruijn-Newman representation of the xi function and investigate whether a structural invariant of the heat flow forces the relevant Jensen/Hermite determinants to retain the required sign all the way to the physical parameter. Any such monotonicity statement must be proved, not inferred numerically.

## Route C: generating functions

Search for an all-degree identity connecting the Jensen polynomial J_{d,n} to derivatives or coefficient extractions of a single entire function in a way that makes real-rootedness transparent. The desired result must cover every d and n simultaneously.

## Route D: Hermite determinant SOS

For each degree, express the full Hermite criterion in centered invariants and search for a degree-uniform sum-of-squares decomposition whose coefficients can be certified directly from the Riemann theta expansion.

## Falsification rule

Any proposed lemma must first be tested against arbitrary positive atomic measures. If it survives only because of an unverified assumption about the Riemann kernel, that assumption must be isolated explicitly as the actual theorem to prove.

## Status

These are research routes, not completed proofs. The universal Riemann-specific closure implication remains the central missing step for RH.
