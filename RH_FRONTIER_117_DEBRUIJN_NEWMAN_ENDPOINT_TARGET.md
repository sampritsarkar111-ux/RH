# Frontier 117 — de Bruijn–Newman endpoint target

Date: 2026-09-17

## Goal

Turn the deformation criterion into an exact zero-independent proof gate rather than numerical evidence about the constant.

## Required theorem

For the standard de Bruijn–Newman family \(H_\lambda\), derive a theta-functional identity or variational inequality that forces the endpoint condition equivalent to RH.

A useful certificate would have the form
\[
\mathcal E_\lambda[f]=\|A_\lambda f\|^2+R_\lambda[f],\qquad R_\lambda[f]\ge0,
\]
with a rigorous implication from the sign/rigidity of \(\mathcal E_\lambda\) to the required endpoint inequality.

## Anti-circularity

The construction cannot use the zero set or assume the critical-line location of any zero. Bounds or numerical approximations for the de Bruijn–Newman constant are not completion.

## Failure rule

If the proposed identity yields only one-sided bounds insufficient for the RH-equivalent endpoint condition, classify it OPEN rather than upgrading it to a proof.
