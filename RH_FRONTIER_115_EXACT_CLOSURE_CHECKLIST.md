# RH Frontier 115 — Exact Closure Checklist

Date: 2026-09-16

The program can only be promoted to a proof after all of the following are established without assuming RH:

- Exact definition and normalization of the completed xi/theta kernel.
- Exact all-degree Jensen/Hermite criterion connecting the kernel to real-rootedness.
- A theta-specific stability, total-positivity, or equivalent variation-diminishing theorem.
- Explicit treatment of the zero mode.
- Exact modular boundary accounting.
- Convergence and interchange justification for every sum/integral used.
- Positivity of every claimed remainder for the full parameter domain.
- A theorem showing that the positive object actually forces hyperbolicity.
- No use of critical-line zero parametrizations before the conclusion.
- No finite-degree or numerical extrapolation in place of the all-degree theorem.

Current state: the first several structural reductions are available in the RH repository and Supabase ledger, but the central all-degree root-location theorem remains open. Therefore a complete proof cannot honestly be reported yet.
