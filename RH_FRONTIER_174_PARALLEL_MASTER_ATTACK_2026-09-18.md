# Frontier 174 — Parallel Master Attack on All Remaining RH Gates — 2026-09-18

## Objective
Attempt every remaining proof-bearing route in parallel. No route is declared complete unless it yields an unconditional theorem implying Re(rho)=1/2 for every nontrivial zeta zero.

## A. Central Stieltjes/Pick gate
Set Xi(w)=xi(1/2+w), H(z)=Xi(sqrt(z)), F(z)=H'(z)/H(z).
Target:
F(z)=a+integral_[0,infinity] dmu(t)/(z+t), mu>=0.
Equivalent interior sign target:
Im F(z)<=0 for Im z>0, together with the required analyticity, normalization, and growth hypotheses.
If proved, factorial-Hankel positivity follows by an exact Gram representation.

## B. Theta-to-sign attack
Use the completed xi/theta integral representation to rewrite H and H'/H. Seek a direct representation of the imaginary part as a negative quadratic/energy expression. Any identity must remain valid without assuming zero locations.
First obstruction test: numerical or symbolic sign failure at any upper-half-plane point kills this exact Stieltjes target.

## C. All-order Hankel/SOS attack
Derive factorial moments from the theta kernel and seek a Binet-Cauchy/Gram decomposition valid for every matrix order N. Finite PSD determinants are diagnostics only. The proof-bearing object must be an explicit positive measure or exact sum/integral of squares.

## D. Jensen/Hermite attack
Translate Xi derivatives into Jensen polynomials and Hermite matrices. Derive exact degree-4 inequalities and search for a structural all-degree total-positivity mechanism. Eventual hyperbolicity is insufficient; the target is every degree and every shift needed to force the full Laguerre-Pólya property.

## E. de Branges/Hilbert-Pólya attack
Require simultaneously: explicit theta-defined Hilbert space; densely defined symmetric operator; self-adjoint realization; discrete spectrum; exact spectral determinant or canonical-system determinant equal to Xi up to an explicitly fixed nonvanishing factor; and a rigorous zero/spectrum correspondence. Reject weighted first-order operators that conjugate to free momentum.

## F. Li/Weil attack
Write the exact Weil quadratic form for the completed zeta function and seek a universal theta/arithmetic Gram factorization. Positivity must hold for the complete admissible test class, not merely for selected test functions or finitely many Li coefficients.

## G. de Bruijn-Newman attack
Translate RH into Lambda<=0 and seek an RH-independent theta-kernel estimate that controls the endpoint. Any estimate proving only Lambda>=0 or finite-time deformed reality does not close RH.

## H. Nyman-Beurling/Baez-Duarte attack
Formulate the exact Hilbert-space distance/closure statement and identify a universal constructive density or coercivity inequality. Finite approximations are not sufficient.

## I. Recent claimed-proof audit
SciSpace search found recent manuscripts claiming RH proofs through function theory, spectral purity, Clark representations, distributional symmetry defects, and operator packages. These remain unverified research claims; abstracts are not accepted as proofs. In particular, a terminal theorem conditional on an unproved determinant/Herglotz certificate does not close RH.

## J. Structural no-go retained
Functional-equation symmetry alone is insufficient. Exact Wolfram check for f(s)=(s-1/2)^2-1 gives f(1-s)=f(s), conjugation symmetry, zeros -1/2 and 3/2, while f(1/2+it)=-1-t^2. Thus symmetry plus reality on the critical line does not force critical-line zeros.

## Acceptance criterion
A COMPLETE status requires a self-contained, unconditional proof of RH. Numerical evidence, finite certificates, conditional theorem wrappers, operator ansatzes without determinant identification, or positivity statements defined from the unknown zeros do not qualify.
