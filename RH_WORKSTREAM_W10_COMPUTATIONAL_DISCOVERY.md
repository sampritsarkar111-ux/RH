# W10 — Computational Discovery Protocol

Compute high-precision/exact candidates for:

- \(\tau_n\);
- Hankel determinants and principal minors;
- Jensen discriminants and root patterns;
- theta-kernel integrals;
- candidate Gram/Vandermonde residuals;
- continued-fraction/Jacobi parameters.

Use computation only to discover or falsify identities. Every candidate entering the proof must receive an exact symbolic derivation and an analytic convergence proof. No numerical positivity claim is itself an RH proof.
