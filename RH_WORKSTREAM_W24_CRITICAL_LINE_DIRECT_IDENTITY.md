# W24 — Direct Critical-Line Identity Route

Search for an identity involving Xi(z), its theta kernel, or a canonical transform that forces every zero to lie on the real spectral axis without first proving Hankel PSD.

The required form is an exact implication from a zero-location-free analytic identity to \(\Re\rho=1/2\). Any factorization involving \(|\Xi|^2\), real zero products, or conjugate pairing must be justified independently and cannot presuppose RH.
