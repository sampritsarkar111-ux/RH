# Frontier 130 — Master plan to an unconditional Riemann Hypothesis proof

Date: 2026-09-17

## Executive status

The Riemann Hypothesis is still officially listed by the Clay Mathematics Institute as an unsolved problem. This project therefore has no completed proof to report.

Two different percentages must be kept separate:

- **Formal proof completion: 0/100.** The central all-degree theta-specific stability theorem is not proved, so the RH implication is not yet established.
- **Progress on this particular proof architecture: about 30/100.** This is a project-management estimate based on the number and weight of verified mathematical gates already closed. It is not a probability that RH will be solved, and it is not a claim that “30% of the theorem” is known.

The remaining 70 points are deliberately concentrated around the genuinely unresolved all-degree bridge rather than spread artificially across bookkeeping tasks.

## I. What is already established in this project

### A. Exact completed-theta representation — CLOSED

The completed kernel is retained as a full lattice sum,
\[
\Phi(u)=\sum_{m\ge1}\phi_m(u),
\]
with the completion symmetry \(\Phi(-u)=\Phi(u)\).

The coefficient sequence is represented exactly by
\[
M_k=\int_{\mathbb R}u^{2k}\Phi(u)\,du,
\qquad
\gamma_k=\frac{M_k}{(2k)!}.
\]
No zero location is used.

### B. Factorial diagonal operator — CLOSED

For each shift \(n\),
\[
\mathcal T_n[z^j]
=\lambda_j^{(n)}z^j,
\qquad
\lambda_j^{(n)}=\frac{(2n)!}{(2n+2j)!}.
\]
Its exponential generating function is
\[
{}_0F_2\!\left(;n+\tfrac12,n+1;z/4\right).
\]
The reciprocal-gamma/Laguerre multiplier route gives an unconditional all-degree stability theorem for the factorial symbol
\[
G_{n,d}(x,y)=\sum_{j=0}^{d}\binom dj\lambda_j^{(n)}x^jy^{d-j}.
\]

### C. Finite-degree exact gates — CLOSED AS ALGEBRAIC CRITERIA

Degree 2:
\[
\gamma_{n+1}^2\ge\gamma_n\gamma_{n+2}.
\]

Degree 3:
\[
P_n\le1,
\qquad
|Q_n-(3P_n-2)|\le2(1-P_n)^{3/2}.
\]

Degree 4 and selected higher finite tests have been reduced to explicit discriminant/resultant conditions.

### D. Failed generic routes — CLOSED AS NO-GO RESULTS

The project has exact obstructions to several tempting mechanisms:

1. single-Vandermonde/Andreief factorisation of the factorial-corrected theta integrand;
2. positive moment-measure positivity as a sufficient condition for Jensen hyperbolicity;
3. generic positive integration of pointwise stable bivariate polynomials;
4. several zero-tail arguments that become circular because they encode zero locations before RH is known.

These are valuable eliminations: future derivations must not reuse the same invalid implication under new notation.

# II. The central unresolved theorem

The exact bivariate unsummed theta/factorial symbol is
\[
\boxed{
\Sigma_{n,d}(x,y)
=
\frac1{(2n)!}
\int_{\mathbb R}
\Phi(u)u^{2n}G_{n,d}(u^2x,y)\,du
}
\]
with
\[
G_{n,d}(x,y)
=
\sum_{j=0}^{d}inom dj
\frac{(2n)!}{(2n+2j)!}x^jy^{d-j}.
\]

The factorial symbol \(G_{n,d}\) is stable. The missing statement is
\[
\boxed{\Sigma_{n,d}\text{ is stable for every }n,d.}
\]
This is equivalent to hyperbolicity of every Jensen polynomial generated by the completed Xi coefficients.

A proof of this statement must use a theta-specific structural mechanism; positivity of \(\Phi\) alone is insufficient.

# III. Work plan — execute all tracks in parallel

## Track 1 — Exact Poisson/Jacobi-transformed symbol

### Target
Keep the lattice unsummed through the modular transformation and derive an exact transformed bivariate symbol
\[
\widetilde\Sigma_{n,d}(x,y;t)
\]
whose stability or failure can be checked algebraically.

### Steps

1. Write every \(\phi_m(u)\) contribution in a variable adapted to \(e^{2u}\).
2. Apply the theta/Poisson transformation before integrating the global moment.
3. Introduce the bivariate stability variables only after the transformed lattice is explicit.
4. Separate factors that are manifestly stable from the genuinely mixed remainder.
5. Compute the imaginary-part elimination condition for \(x,y\in\mathbb H\).
6. Search for an exact factorization of the boundary expression into
   \[
   \sum_r |P_r|^2,
   \]
   a determinant square, or a Vandermonde square multiplied by a nonnegative modular factor.
7. Test the construction at \(d=2,3,4\) exactly before attempting all \(d\).

### Advance criterion
A factorization valid symbolically for arbitrary integer \(d\), not just numerical positivity for selected \(d\).

### Failure criterion
An exact complex specialization \((x,y,n,d)\) producing a zero in the forbidden domain, or an exact sign-indefinite residual that cannot be removed by the allowed symmetry.

## Track 2 — Hermite/Gram route

### Target
Convert the full Hermite matrix of each Jensen polynomial into an explicit PSD Gram object.

### Steps

1. Start with the monic degree-\(d\) Jensen polynomial.
2. Express Newton power sums through the coefficient list \(\gamma_n,\ldots,\gamma_{n+d}\).
3. Insert the multi-copy moment representation.
4. Keep every lattice copy explicit.
5. Symmetrise over copies before attempting factorisation.
6. Search for a universal antisymmetric factor
   \[
   V(u_1,\ldots,u_q)^2
   \]
   only after all factorial couplings are included.
7. Determine whether the remainder is a PSD kernel, a sum of squares, or a Pfaffian/determinantal square.
8. Prove the construction for arbitrary \(d\), not merely cubic and quartic cases.

### Advance criterion

\[
 c^T H_{d,n}c
=
\int |P_{d,n,c}|^2\,d\mu_{d,n}
\ge0
\]
with every factor explicitly defined and no assumption about Xi zeros.

### Failure criterion
A finite-degree principal minor or residual is exactly negative for the proposed universal Gram ansatz.

---

## Track 3 — Stability-preserving integral operator

### Target
Find a theorem stronger than ordinary positive averaging that acts specifically on the family
\[
G_{n,d}(u^2x,y).
\]

### Candidate mechanisms

1. common proper-position / interlacing structure;
2. variation-diminishing integral transforms;
3. total positivity of a transformed two-variable kernel;
4. multivariate Pólya-Schur symbol composition;
5. finite-rank stable-kernel decomposition;
6. differential-operator representation of the theta transform;
7. determinantal stability via a block operator rather than scalar averaging.

### Hard requirement
The theorem must be proved for the actual theta family. A theorem saying “positive combinations preserve stability” is forbidden because it is false in general.

### Advance criterion
A self-contained operator theorem that maps the entire family of factorial symbols to stable outputs.

---

## Track 4 — Theta modular remainder extraction

### Target
Locate the genuinely Riemann-specific term that generic positive-moment arguments cannot see.

### Steps

1. Express the reflection \(u\mapsto-u\) at the unsummed lattice level.
2. Pair lattice contributions only after the exact modular relation is written.
3. Expand the paired contribution algebraically.
4. Separate symmetric positive pieces from the signed remainder.
5. Determine whether the remainder equals a derivative-free square, a theta-derivative square, a positive integral, or an exactly controlled telescoping boundary term.
6. Repeat at degree 2, 3, 4.
7. Identify the pattern in \(d\), then prove the general induction or determinant identity.

### Advance criterion
An explicit nonnegative modular remainder that vanishes or has a controlled sign in every degree.

---

## Track 5 — Exact finite-degree adversarial testing

Every proposed mechanism is to be attacked before it is generalized.

### Required tests

- \(d=2\): exact discriminant;
- \(d=3\): exact cubic discriminant and Hermite determinant;
- \(d=4\): exact discriminant plus principal Hermite minors;
- selected \(d=5,6,7,8,10\): exact rational arithmetic where computationally feasible;
- arbitrary positive measures as adversarial controls;
- synthetic non-Riemannian theta-like measures to distinguish generic from genuinely theta-specific structure.

### Rule
Numerical success is evidence only. Any proposed theorem must survive an exact symbolic adversarial test.

## Track 6 — Independent multiplier-sequence investigation

The factorial side is already strongly constrained, but the generating function
\[
\Psi_n(z)={}_0F_2\!\left(;n+\tfrac12,n+1;z/4\right)
\]
should be independently audited.

### Steps

1. Derive its differential equation.
2. Search for an integral or product representation with manifest zero control.
3. Derive recurrence relations for its Jensen polynomials.
4. Compare with known hyperbolicity-preserving transforms.
5. Prove exactly which part follows from reciprocal-gamma sampling and which part needs an independent argument.

This track cannot be used as a substitute for the theta bridge.

## Track 7 — Zero-free final bridge

After, and only after, proving all-degree Jensen hyperbolicity independently of zeros:

1. Establish the required Jensen/Pólya criterion in the exact normalization used here.
2. Prove that all Jensen polynomials are hyperbolic.
3. Deduce that the associated entire function belongs to the relevant Laguerre–Pólya class.
4. Identify the associated entire function with the completed Xi function.
5. Translate real-zero structure of Xi into
   \[
   \Re\rho=\frac12
   \]
   for every nontrivial zero \(\rho\) of \(\zeta(s)\).
6. Audit every implication for hidden use of zero locations.

This is the terminal bridge to RH.

# IV. Decision tree after the next exact computations

## Case A — transformed theta symbol is stable

Immediately attempt:

\[
\text{finite }d
\rightarrow
\text{pattern}
\rightarrow
\text{all-degree identity}
\rightarrow
\text{operator theorem}
\rightarrow
\text{Jensen hyperbolicity}
\rightarrow RH.
\]

The key priority is proving the pattern for arbitrary \(d\), not collecting more numerical examples.

## Case B — transformed symbol fails

Do not patch the same operator. Extract the exact residual
\[
R_{n,d}(x,y)
\]
and classify it:

- positive plus negative square;
- modular boundary term;
- missing cross-copy interaction;
- wrong normalization;
- wrong operator class.

Then construct the smallest replacement operator that cancels that residual.

## Case C — a Gram factorization exists only at low degree

Treat it as a discovery mechanism, not a proof. Determine the coefficient pattern and identify the degree-dependent object whose generalization would be required.

## Case D — a general stability theorem is found for an enlarged operator

Replace \(\mathcal T_n\) by the enlarged operator and repeat the full symbol classification. The objective is not loyalty to the current ansatz; it is closure of the theta-to-Jensen map.

# V. Proof invariants — rules that cannot be violated

1. **No RH assumption.** Never assume the Xi zeros are real.
2. **No zero parametrization.** Do not write \(\rho=1/2+i\gamma\) as a premise.
3. **No circular dominant-zero asymptotics.** Any tail estimate must be zero-location independent.
4. **No generic positive-integration claim.** Stability of each integrand is not enough.
5. **No numerical-to-symbolic promotion.** Numerical roots are evidence only.
6. **No hidden normalization changes.** Track every \((2n)!\), binomial coefficient, and shift.
7. **No single-degree extrapolation without a degree-uniform identity.**
8. **Every “positive” remainder must be shown PSD or pointwise nonnegative by an exact formula.**
9. **Every use of Poisson/Jacobi must occur before any step that would destroy the relevant modular cancellation.**
10. **Every terminal RH implication must be written separately from the hyperbolicity proof.**

# VII. Definition of “complete proof” for this project

The project may only be marked COMPLETE when all of the following are simultaneously proved:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&\text{(1) Exact unsummed theta identity;}\\
&\text{(2) exact theta-specific stability/Gram mechanism;}\\
&\text{(3) validity for every }n,d;\\
&\text{(4) no-zero-location assumptions anywhere in (1)-(3); }\\
&\text{(5) all-degree Jensen hyperbolicity;}\\
&\text{(6) rigorous Jensen/Pólya passage to Xi in the LP class;}\\
&\text{(7) zero-free deduction of }\Re\rho=1/2;\\
&\text{(8) independent symbolic/numerical consistency checks.}
\end{aligned}}
\]

Until all eight are closed, the repository must say “RH remains unproved.”
