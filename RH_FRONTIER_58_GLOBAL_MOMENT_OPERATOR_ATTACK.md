# Frontier 58 — global moment-operator attack on the remaining Turan gate

## Status
Active research frontier. No RH conclusion is claimed.

## Exact target
Let μ_j be the moments of the specific positive theta measure and define M_m=16m(2m-1)μ_{m-1}-μ_m and r_j=μ_{j+1}/μ_j. Then M_m=μ_{m-1}(16m(2m-1)-r_{m-1}). The factorial-conjugate Turan inequality is equivalent to the corresponding three-ratio inequality in r_{m-1},r_m,r_{m+1}.

## Adversarial result
Ordinary Stieltjes moment facts, including r_{m-1}≤r_m≤r_{m+1}, are insufficient to prove this inequality. Thus the remaining closure must exploit structure specific to the theta measure.

## New operator target
For ⟨p,q⟩=∫p(y)q(y)dσ(y), seek an explicit theta-specific polynomial/difference operator L_m and Gram decomposition of the Turan defect T_m=M_{m+1}²-c_m M_mM_{m+2}. The desired form is T_m=⟨L_m p_m,p_m⟩+Σ_j c_{m,j}||q_{m,j}||² with c_{m,j}≥0, where the theta differential/difference equation and modular involution control L_m.

## High-value subtargets
1. Prove a theta-specific three-ratio inequality.
2. Produce a positive-semidefinite Hankel/Gram identity for T_m.
3. Derive a finite-order theta-moment recurrence with nonnegative remainder.
4. Derive a modularly symmetrized sum-of-squares decomposition.

No generic log-convexity, atomwise positivity, numerical verification, or asymptotic estimate is accepted as final closure. Higher-degree Jensen hyperbolicity and the final equivalence to RH remain separate proof obligations.
