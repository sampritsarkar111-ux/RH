# W14 — Total Positivity / PF∞ Route

Test whether the coefficient sequence of the symmetric completed Xi function, or a canonical transform of it, satisfies an all-order Pólya-frequency/total-positivity condition that implies Jensen hyperbolicity and hence RH.

Targets:
- identify the exact Toeplitz/Hankel matrix;
- derive its minors from theta data;
- prove all minors nonnegative without assuming RH;
- distinguish necessary conditions from an actual Laguerre–Pólya theorem.

Any finite collection of positive minors is only a diagnostic.
