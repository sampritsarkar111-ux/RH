# W19 — Low-Degree-to-Structure Discovery

Compute exact d=2 and d=3 theta-derived Hermite/Hankel forms, factor them symbolically, and search for invariants that persist with d. Candidate invariants include Vandermonde squares, Schur polynomials, determinant identities, reflection pairs, and trace-class operator factorizations.

The purpose is theorem discovery: any observed pattern must then be proved for arbitrary degree independently.
