# Frontier 66 — PF5 obstruction and route change

Date: 2026-09-16

## New external mathematical constraint

A 2026 arXiv preprint by Wojciech Michałowski reports a rigorously interval-certified negative 5x5 Toeplitz minor for the classical de Bruijn-Newman kernel at a specific configuration, while lower orders 2–4 are positive there. The paper states that this establishes failure of PF5 for that kernel configuration, while the global PF4 question remains open. This is an external result and has not been independently reproduced here.

## Consequence for this project

Do not pursue a proof of RH based on the unsupported assumption that the raw Riemann kernel is PF_infinity. Such an assumption would be false if the reported PF5 counterexample is correct. Any total-positivity route must use a transformed kernel/operator for which the required positivity theorem is actually true, or use a different global mechanism.

## Revised target

Investigate:

1. Jensen-polynomial/Hermite positivity of the coefficient sequence rather than raw kernel PF_infinity;
2. heat-flow spectral inequalities that do not require PF_infinity of the untransformed kernel;
3. Weil-functional positivity and operator coercivity;
4. transformed or finite-dimensional kernels whose positivity implies Xi in Laguerre-Pólya through a valid theorem.

## Status

This is a strategic falsification/update, not a proof or disproof of RH.
