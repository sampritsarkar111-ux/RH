# W26 — Hilbert/Hankel Operator Route

Investigate whether the sequence tau_n can be realized as matrix elements of a self-adjoint positive operator built directly from the theta kernel:
\[
\tau_n=\langle e_0,T^n e_0\rangle,
\qquad T\ge0.
\]
If such a representation is proved unconditionally, Hankel PSD follows immediately from
\[
Q_N(c)=\left\|\sum_{j=0}^N c_jT^{j/2}e_0\right\|^2.
\]
The hard requirement is constructing T and proving positivity without assuming RH.
