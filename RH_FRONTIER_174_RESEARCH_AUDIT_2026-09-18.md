# Frontier 174 — Research Audit and Conditional-Proof Filter — 2026-09-18

SciSpace returned recent manuscripts claiming RH proofs via function-theoretic boundary-to-interior arguments, spectral constructions, Clark representations, distributional symmetry defects, and operator packages. Their abstracts describe terminal conclusions but also expose explicit dependencies such as Carleson-box inequalities, determinant identification, Herglotz/Hermite–Biehler certificates, or atom-freeness/coercivity statements.

Project rule: an abstract-level claim is not imported as a theorem. Each claimed proof must be audited at the exact load-bearing lemma. If the final result is conditional on an unproved determinant identity, positivity certificate, or measure property, it remains conditional.

This prevents the project from treating a proposed route as closed merely because a manuscript uses the words 'proof' or 'complete'.
