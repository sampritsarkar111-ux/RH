# Frontier 37 — degree-3 exact condition in consecutive xi coefficients

Frontier 36 gives
\[
\Delta_3=\frac{d^3(d-1)^2(d-2)}{12}\,\widetilde Q_d.
\]
At \(d=3\), exact symbolic factorization gives
\[
\boxed{\widetilde Q_3=-3w^2-12v^3},
\]
where
\[
v=\frac{q_2q_0-q_1^2}{q_0^2},
\qquad
w=\frac{q_3q_0^2-3q_1q_2q_0+2q_1^3}{q_0^3}.
\]
Since the prefactor at \(d=3\) equals \(9\),
\[
\boxed{\Delta_3(d=3)=-27\,(w^2+4v^3).}
\]
Therefore the exact Hermite positivity condition is
\[
\boxed{w^2+4v^3\le0.}
\]
Because \(w^2\ge0\), this forces
\[
\boxed{v\le0},
\]
and more strongly
\[
\boxed{-4v^3\ge w^2}.
\]

In terms of the original consecutive coefficients, the first condition is
\[
\boxed{q_0q_2-q_1^2\le0}.
\]
Thus the degree-3 Hermite condition forces adjacent log-concavity at this location. The stronger inequality is a cubic discriminant-type constraint on the same five coefficients.

For the Riemann-xi sequence this is an exact five-consecutive-coefficient condition. The next target is to insert
\(\gamma_m=C_0m!M_m/(2m)!\) and derive these signs directly from the exact theta kernel.

**Status:** exact necessary condition; no RH proof.
