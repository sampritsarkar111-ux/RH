# Frontier 118 — Final adversarial RH proof audit checklist

Date: 2026-09-17

A manuscript may be labeled a complete RH proof only if every item below is discharged explicitly.

1. Exact definition of \(\xi\) and \(\Xi\).
2. Exact theta representation with normalization checked.
3. All exchanges of sums, integrals, derivatives, and limits justified.
4. No assumption that an unknown zero has real part \(1/2\).
5. No operator or measure defined using the unknown zero set.
6. Infinite-order positivity or another theorem genuinely sufficient for zero-realness.
7. Multiplicities handled.
8. Analytic continuation and domains handled.
9. Any spectral identification is exact, not heuristic.
10. Any Pick/Herglotz representation has the correct meromorphic-domain logic.
11. Any Jensen criterion is applied in its exact normalization.
12. Numerical calculations are explicitly separated from proof.
13. Every auxiliary lemma is either proved or cited as a standard theorem.
14. The final implication is written explicitly:
\[
\zeta(\rho)=0,\quad 0<\Re\rho<1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\Re\rho=\tfrac12.
\]

Until every item is discharged, the status is OPEN, regardless of numerical agreement or the number of successful finite-degree tests.
