# Frontier 51 — Universal endgame statement

Date: 2026-09-16

## Exact endgame

The Jensen-polynomial route has a single decisive endpoint:
\[
\boxed{J_{d,n}(X)=\sum_{j=0}^{d}\binom dj\gamma(n+j)X^j\text{ is hyperbolic for every }d,n\ge0.}
\]

The finite Hermite calculations are reductions of this statement, not replacements for it.

## What a complete proof must supply

A theorem, derived from the specific Riemann xi/theta structure, that implies the full Hermite hyperbolicity criterion uniformly in both indices. The theorem may use total positivity, a Laguerre-Pólya representation, a heat-flow invariant, a determinant identity, or another rigorous mechanism, but every implication must be proved.

## Current exact bottleneck

The degree-3 condition is the centered cubic cone 4(-v)^3>=w^2. The degree-4 condition is the normalized cubic F(y,t)>=0 together with the lower Hermite conditions. These reveal that the missing information is not ordinary positivity of the theta moment measure: it is a stronger structural constraint on the Riemann coefficient sequence.

## No false closure

A proof cannot be declared complete from numerical verification, finite-degree calculations, asymptotic hyperbolicity, or an unproved assertion that the theta kernel is totally positive. The universal theorem itself remains the object that must be established.

## Status

Endgame precisely isolated. RH remains unproved in this research record.
