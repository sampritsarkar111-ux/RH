# Frontier 11 — Spectral Route Audit

## Target

A Hilbert–Pólya-type route would seek a self-adjoint operator \(H\) with a rigorously defined determinant satisfying an exact identity of the form

\[
\det_{\!\mathrm{reg}}(H-z)=C\,\xi\!\left(\frac12+z\right)
\]

for a nonzero normalization constant \(C\), or an equivalent canonical spectral determinant identity.

## Non-negotiable conditions

1. Define the Hilbert space and dense operator domain.
2. Prove symmetry and self-adjointness (or an exact self-adjoint extension statement).
3. Establish the relevant spectral discreteness or a rigorous regularization scheme.
4. Prove the determinant identity with \(\xi\), including zeros and multiplicities.
5. Only then infer that zeros correspond to real spectral parameters.

## Rejection tests

The following are not sufficient:

- matching a few eigenvalue spacings;
- a formal differential operator with no self-adjointness proof;
- a trace formula whose convergence is unproved;
- a determinant identity asserted only asymptotically;
- a physical analogy without a mathematical spectral equivalence.

## Research direction

Compare the explicit theta-kernel representation with candidate integral, differential, or transfer operators. Search for an exact symmetry that forces a real spectrum while preserving the completed zeta functional equation.

## Status

This is a research program, not a proof. No self-adjoint operator satisfying all conditions has been established in this repository.
