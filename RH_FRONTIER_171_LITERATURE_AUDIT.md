# Frontier 171 Literature Audit — 2026-09-17

## Important search result
SciSpace returned several recent Zenodo-indexed manuscripts whose abstracts claim complete proofs of RH. These claims are not sufficient evidence of a solved Millennium problem merely because they appear in an academic index. The current official Clay Mathematics Institute page still labels RH **Unsolved**.

## Useful rigorous references found
- Rodgers–Tao, *The De Bruijn-Newman constant is non-negative* (2018): establishes Lambda >= 0. RH is equivalent to Lambda <= 0. Thus this route still needs the opposite endpoint inequality.
- Baez-Duarte, *A strengthening of the Nyman-Beurling criterion for the Riemann Hypothesis* (2002): RH is equivalent to the closure statement involving the natural-number-generated subspace. This is an equivalence, not a proof of the closure.
- Baez-Duarte, *A general strong Nyman-Beurling Criterion for the Riemann Hypothesis* (2005): gives further equivalence/criterion results; again the difficult closure implication remains the proof target.

## Unverified claimed-proof papers
SciSpace also surfaced manuscripts claiming complete RH proofs through CSP/global-bridge, robustness/heat-flow, or operator programs. Because Clay still lists RH as unsolved, these claims must be treated as unverified until every theorem and hypothesis is independently checked. They are not promoted into the project as solved results.

## Consequence for Frontier 171
The correct next move is not to copy claimed proof conclusions. It is to extract each claimed terminal inequality/identity and independently prove its analytic hypotheses, looking especially for hidden RH dependence, circular definitions, missing limit arguments, or unjustified positivity transfers.
