# Frontier 65 — Master next-target stack for a complete RH proof attempt

Date: 2026-09-16

## Parallel targets

### A. Theta-kernel global positivity
Derive an exact inequality from the explicit Riemann theta/Jacobi kernel that is strong enough to imply Lambda <= 0 or an equivalent Laguerre-Pólya criterion.

### B. Heat-flow route
Analyze Xi_lambda under the de Bruijn-Newman heat equation and seek a rigorously signed Lyapunov/dissipation quantity valid down to lambda=0.

### C. Jensen/Hermite route
Extend centered invariant calculations to degrees 5, 6, ... and identify a recurrence or matrix-positivity theorem that is uniform in d and n.

### D. Finite-strip closure
The strongest known asymptotic wedge still leaves a finite-strip regime. Any complete attempt must explicitly close this regime rather than hide it in an asymptotic limit.

### E. Weil-positivity route
Investigate whether the explicit-formula/Weil criterion admits a global operator coercivity estimate that can be proved directly from the prime and archimedean terms.

### F. Independent audit
Every candidate proof is to be checked for:
- quantifier completeness;
- analytic convergence and interchange;
- infinite-tail bounds;
- degenerate Hermite cases;
- dependence on unproved RH-equivalent assumptions;
- numerical-versus-analytic gaps.

## Acceptance condition

The ledger may be marked COMPLETE only after a rigorous argument establishes RH itself. Otherwise every frontier remains PARTIAL, regardless of numerical success or the number of derived identities.

## Current assessment

The program has produced exact algebraic reductions and sharper proof targets, but the decisive universal theorem is still missing. The next work should prioritize discovering that theorem rather than generating more isolated low-degree identities.
