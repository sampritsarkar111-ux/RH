# W1 — Exact Zero-Free Extraction of tau_n

Work from the completed Xi function and its symmetric variable. Establish the exact Taylor/logarithmic-derivative identities for
\[
v_\rho=-(\rho-1/2)^{-2},\qquad \tau_n=\sum_\rho v_\rho^{n+1},
\]
without assuming any reality or positivity of \(v_\rho\).

Targets:
- prove absolute/local convergence needed for each expansion;
- derive the exact coefficient relation from the Hadamard product;
- track multiplicities and the possible accumulation at \(v=0\);
- provide a zero-location-free formula usable in W2–W4.

This workstream supplies the exact analytic input; it must not smuggle in RH through a transformed-variable positivity statement.
