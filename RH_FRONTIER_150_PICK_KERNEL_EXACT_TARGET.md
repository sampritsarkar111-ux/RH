# Frontier 150A — Exact Global Pick Target

Let Xi denote the completed Riemann Xi function and let H be the square-root-variable normalization used by the project. The pointwise theta-derived kernel appearing in Im(H'/H) is sign-indefinite, so pointwise positivity is unavailable.

## Proof-bearing target
Retain the full unsummed theta lattice in the double-integral representation of the Pick numerator. Apply the Poisson/Jacobi transformation to the complete lattice block before any collapse. Seek an identity

P(z)=S(z)^*S(z)+R(z),

for Im(z)>0, with R(z)>=0 and all definitions independent of the zeros of Xi.

## Required audit
1. Derive the exact transformed lattice expression.
2. Pair all indices explicitly and track signs.
3. Determine whether the antisymmetric terms cancel or leave a residual.
4. If a square factorization exists, prove it for finite lattice truncations.
5. Establish locally uniform convergence and preservation of nonnegativity.
6. If impossible, produce a symbolic counterexample/no-go theorem for the proposed algebraic class.

## Forbidden shortcut
Do not insert a representation of zeros as 1/2+it, do not define a measure from zeros, and do not infer the Pick sign from RH-equivalent positivity.

## Acceptance
A valid global Pick/Stieltjes inequality would provide a new RH-independent mechanism. Without that inequality, this route remains open.
