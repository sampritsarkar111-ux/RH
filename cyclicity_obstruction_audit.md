# Cyclicity obstruction audit

## Exact observation
For the arithmetic synthesis family on the Mellin side, finite vectors have the form

F_N(s)=zeta(s) A_N(s)/s,

where A_N is a Dirichlet polynomial. If zeta(rho)=0 at a point rho in a Hardy half-plane where evaluation is continuous, then F_N(rho)=0 for every N. The closure of the family therefore remains in the kernel of evaluation at rho, while the target 1/s has value 1/rho. Hence the target cannot be cyclic.

This gives the rigorous obstruction

zeta(rho)=0, Re(rho)>1/2 => failure of the corresponding Hardy cyclicity.

In the critical L2 Nyman–Beurling formulation, Baéz-Duarte proved

chi_(0,1) in closure span{rho_a:a in N} <=> RH.

Therefore proving the requested cyclicity unconditionally would itself prove RH. The finite positive Gram identity is exact but cannot logically imply cyclicity by positivity alone.

## What a genuine breakthrough must add
A successful proof must establish an unconditional theorem that removes every possible adjoint-null functional / evaluation obstruction without presupposing zero-freeness of zeta in Re(s)>1/2. Equivalently, it must supply a new mechanism beyond finite Gram positivity, such as a genuinely new global arithmetic factorization or a new completeness theorem.

No RH proof is claimed in this audit.
